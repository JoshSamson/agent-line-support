# Agent Line privacy policy

*Effective 18 November 2026. Published at https://github.com/JoshSamson/agent-line-support/blob/main/PRIVACY.md and inside the app under Settings, Privacy.*

Agent Line is an iPhone app for calling voice agents that run on a server you choose. It is published by Joshua Todes.

## What the publisher collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information to the publisher.

## What stays on your phone

- The servers you add: each one's name, address and secret, kept in the iPhone Keychain.
- Your call history: which agent, when and for how long.
- The photo or icon you choose for an agent.
- Your theme choice.

Removing a server removes its agents' photos and its calls. Deleting the app removes everything.

## What goes to the servers you add

When you add a server, the app asks it for its list of agents. When you call an agent, your voice goes to the call service that server names, so the agent can hear and answer you. Whoever runs that server decides what happens to the audio and to anything you say. The publisher does not run those servers and cannot see your calls.

## Permissions

- Microphone: to speak on a call. Asked the first time you call.
- Camera: to scan a server's pairing code. The image is read on the phone and not kept.
- Photos: you pick a photo for an agent with the system picker. The app sees only the photo you pick.
- Local network: to reach a server on your own network.

## The demo server

The publisher runs a demo server so people can try a call. If you add it and call it, your voice is processed by that server and by the speech and language services it uses, to produce the agent's replies. It does not keep what you say.

## Children

Agent Line is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
